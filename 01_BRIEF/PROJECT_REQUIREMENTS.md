# Project brief — Pizza / Döner / Yufka Lieferservice (Germany)

## Audience & conversion
German neighborhood takeaway/delivery brand, NO seating. Target hungry local customers; quickly understand food types, pickup vs delivery, and CALL. No online order app, no booking, no reviews invented.

## Brand
Brand name unknown. Current visuals use a design-only placeholder `PIZZA · DÖNER`; actual logo/name needed from owner. Prefer no invented logo or flame mascot. Hero approved screenshot visually references a concept with a temporary logo: do not copy it as real brand.

## Information architecture
1. Sticky compact header: brand placeholder; menu, pickup/delivery, contact; DE EN UK; call CTA when configured.
2. Hero cinematic story (long form but disciplined) with product and typographic transitions.
3. Short editorial bold statement separating categories.
4. Product runway: pizza, döner spit, fresh yufka (large imagery / whitespace, no generic 3-card grid).
5. Pizza-box opening scene: dark-to-kraft threshold, steam & box product.
6. One acid-lime impact scene with yufka roll-in; clear contrast/legibility.
7. Menu: categories Pizza / Döner / Yufka / Burger / Snacks / Getränke; actual product list pending, never invent prices.
8. Delivery / pickup: phone-led CTA, real phone/address/hours only once supplied.
9. Final dark CTA with kraft bag and large type; legal footer with linked pages after approved text.

## UX
- Always clear primary call-to-action. No cart, account, payment or booking. Sticky call CTA can be used only when real number exists.
- Native scroll, no scroll hijacking. Reduce animations for low-powered devices and reduced motion.
- No claims about food sourcing/certification if unverified.
- Hero is high-impact but food ordering must be straightforward and content legible.

## Internationalization
- German default `/de`, English `/en`, Ukrainian `/uk`. All translations included as starting copy and may be refined by native speaker before launch.
- All headings/buttons/category labels translatable; locale-aware `lang`, route switch preserving position where practical.
- UK not RU. Use Cyrillic-safe font choices.

## Definition of done (development)
- Working Next dev and production build.
- Three translated routes; all key sections; consistent design tokens; CTA conditional; no placeholder data emitted as real facts.
- Responsive and reduced-motion accessible.
- All assets tracked via manifest; real transparent PNGs as source; no reference screenshot embedded as website background.
- No broken legal links on public launch (draft pages only until proper legal texts are provided).
